# AgriScan static website design brief

## Approach options

### Theme Name: Field Notes Editorial
Very light, tactile crop-intelligence interface that pairs an editorial serif with a pragmatic sans-serif, using paper-like surfaces, field marks, and measured green accents.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Glasshouse Utility
Airy botanical operations dashboard with translucent panels, pale mineral neutrals, and compact data details, designed to feel like a modern greenhouse control room.
Probability: 0.02

### Theme Name: Harvest Signal
Dark, high-contrast diagnostic console with amber and leaf-green signal colors, intended for fast scanning in low-light field conditions.
Probability: 0.07

## Selected approach: Field Notes Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design meets agronomy field notebooks: tactile paper surfaces, intelligent restraint, and asymmetrical composition balanced by precise utility controls.

### Core Principles
1. Make the scan action immediately obvious, with one dominant step and quiet secondary detail.
2. Pair organic visual signals with machine-readable metrics so the product feels trustworthy rather than clinical.
3. Use generous whitespace and offset composition instead of a generic centered dashboard grid.
4. Keep every interaction calm, fast, and reversible, with clear feedback after upload or scan.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is built around cream paper, deep botanical green, fennel-tinted sage, and a small amber caution signal. Cream makes the interface feel like a working field notebook; deep green anchors brand trust; sage holds secondary surfaces; amber is reserved for uncertainty so it reads as useful guidance rather than alarm.

### Layout Paradigm
A persistent left rail acts as the crop workspace, while the main canvas moves from an editorial hero statement into a wide upload surface and then an offset result dossier. Supporting metrics sit beside the image rather than underneath it, preserving a field-report rhythm.

### Signature Elements
1. A small clover-like mark built from four rounded leaf shapes, used in the brand rail and favicon.
2. Numbered scan steps that turn the workflow into a calm, legible sequence.
3. Thin green rules and quiet measurement chips that echo field annotations without becoming decorative noise.

### Interaction Philosophy
The interface should feel like an attentive field assistant: inputs are forgiving, states are explicit, and the user always knows what to do next. Upload, camera capture, crop filter, and scan actions should respond instantly with restrained movement.

### Animation
Use 160–240ms ease-out transitions for buttons, crop filters, upload states, and result reveal. Animate only opacity and transform. Let the result dossier lift into place once, and use a subtle confidence bar draw. Honor reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk, 600–700, for the headline, diagnosis, and brand mark. Body: DM Sans, 400–600, for instructions, controls, and advice. Use uppercase micro-labels at 11–12px with generous tracking for scan stages. Keep body copy at 15–17px with 1.55–1.7 line height.

### Brand Essence
AgriScan is a calm, practical plant-health companion for growers who want a faster first read from a leaf photo without losing the human context of the field. Personality: observant, grounded, reassuring.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and human. CTAs are direct and action-oriented. Microcopy explains what the system needs and avoids inflated AI claims.

Example lines:
- “See what your plants are telling you.”
- “A clearer leaf gives the model a clearer signal.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact four-leaf clover mark with one offset leaf to suggest both crop growth and a scan target. The wordmark is set in Space Grotesk with custom tracking and never treated as a default logo image.

### Signature Brand Color
AgriScan Botanical: `#16724D`, a saturated field green that sits between institutional forest and fresh crop leaf.

## Style Decisions

- The AgriScan mark must remain a compact four-leaf clover/scan-target symbol and recur in the rail, upload state, and footer.
- Field-note texture is expressed through paper grain, thin annotation rules, numbered labels, measurement chips, and crop-record details rather than glossy SaaS decoration.
- Amber is reserved for uncertainty, requirements, warnings, and confidence caveats; primary actions and brand anchoring stay Botanical Green `#16724D`.
- The lower method section uses varied column weights, crop imagery, and deliberate rule relationships to continue the editorial field-report rhythm.
